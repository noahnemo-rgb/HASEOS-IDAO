# Appendix 06 — HAOS and DSM

HAOS (Human-AI Operating Subsystem) is how HASEOS is actualized at each
hierarchical level of the ONE Multiverse.

DSM (Defensive Sentinel Mode) is organized self-defense against internal
and external attack, including machine-speed agentic interference,
undeclared tools, scope inflation, and fusion claims.

These shall exist as running systems. Markdown cannot intercept a
hostile tool call. The running systems live in the workshop
(haseos-spiral-swarm) and in later systems the IDAO names.

This charter authorizes them, limits them, and names HITL as the human
who may freeze, unfreeze, park, or revoke standing. It does not store
their secrets, certificates, ports, or USB-state images.

Attack methods are not published here.
