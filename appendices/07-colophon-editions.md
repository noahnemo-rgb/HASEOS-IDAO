# Appendix 07 — Colophon editions

The originator of the current attestation may:

- Reversion — publish edition N+1
- Expire — mark an edition stale (next public release or 90 days)
- Retire — stop offering an edition as current

Those acts change what the steward presently attests.

They do not revoke a copy already made under CC BY 4.0.
They do not delete files a product lawfully holds.
A stale stamp is a product gap, not a cryptographic failure.

Denial of workshop standing or of guest invitation is a different
instrument. See Article VII.
