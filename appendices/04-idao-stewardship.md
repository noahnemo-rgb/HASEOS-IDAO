# Appendix 04 — IDAO stewardship

Independent = not a department of a product, vendor, state, church, or
workshop. The charter, later rolls, and later treasury are not hosted
inside a SaaS repo.

Autonomous = self-directing under this charter once membership and a
ratified procedure exist. Not remotely piloted by a platform or a guest
model.

Under the Constitution = bound by the law that creates the body. A
steward who cannot be bound is a second sovereign, not an IDAO.

Until transfer, the founding steward holds this repository.
